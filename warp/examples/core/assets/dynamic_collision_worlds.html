Capacity is a design choice

Fixed slots impose a separate particle and contact cap on every world. Packed tables pool capacity across worlds, allowing uneven populations to share reserved space. The total still has a hard limit: global overflow must be detected and handled.

Packing adds work: contact detection runs twice, and tables are compacted. Memory savings alone do not establish a runtime speedup. Measured timings appear only when supplied by the generator.
